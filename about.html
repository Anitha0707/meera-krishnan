Our story

Your family's financial protection advisors

As Chief Insurance Advisors of the Life Insurance Corporation of India, we help families in and around Coimbatore choose the right life, savings, pension, health and cancer-protection plans.

We believe insurance should be simple to understand and honestly recommended. Every family is different, so we start by listening — your income, responsibilities, children's goals and retirement dreams — and only then suggest a plan.

  • Personalised, needs-based recommendations
  • Clear explanation of benefits, terms and premiums — in Tamil or English
  • Support with paperwork, renewals and claim assistance
  • Long-term relationship, not a one-time sale
